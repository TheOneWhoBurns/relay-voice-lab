# Ecuador recipient-first pitch war game

Date: 12 September 2026. Status: research and proposed dialogue only; not loaded into the application.

## Recommendation to test

> Hola, de Aló. Le ofrecemos una agenda que atiende el teléfono: el paciente pregunta y deja su cita reservada.

The commercial idea is a spoken route from a patient's question to a confirmed appointment. An appointment system becomes something the patient can converse with, rather than something a staff member must operate on that patient's behalf. This is an offer to evaluate, not a claim that the prospect's current calendar is already integrated.

The opening is for the explicitly AI-labelled browser simulation. For a real, authorized callback, identify the verified callback context and the automated nature of the call first; for example, “Hola, es el sistema de voz de Aló. Solicitó información sobre atención telefónica para su clínica.” Use that sentence only when an actual callback request supports it. Do not invent prior contact. If no source of permission is established, do not dial.

This is a structured tabletop exercise written from eight skeptical recipient positions. The responses below are hypotheses, not customer quotations or findings from interviews. No real businesses were contacted, no sales calls were made and no model-generated conversion scores were used. Success on paper is not proof of good spoken delivery, demand or conversion.

## 1. What the local evidence actually supports

### Existing channels are not a blank slate

[Millennials Centro Estético's contact page](https://www.millennials.ec/contacto) points its booking links to WhatsApp and publishes opening hours that include weekends. [Libella's site](https://www.libella.ec/) lists phone/WhatsApp contacts and a request form with a stated response window. [Bèl's Ecuador page](https://bel.clinic/ecuador/) publishes a telephone number, a contact form and valuation-booking links. These are observations of public contact routes, not evidence of how many calls arrive, go unanswered or become bookings.

Implication: do not open by assuming a clinic closes early, loses telephone leads, has no booking system or needs to abandon WhatsApp. Establish relevance from the recipient's actual response. Public pages are context, not authorization to call.

### Reception is several jobs

An employer-authored [VisionCare reception vacancy in Guayaquil](https://ec.linkedin.com/jobs/view/recepcionista-comercial-at-visioncare-4394952911) combines WhatsApp, phone and in-person reception with appointments, payments, quotations, insurance coordination and follow-up. It is one employer's role description, not a representative labor-market survey.

Implication: the product can take a defined class of conversations. It cannot honestly claim to replace the complete job, save an entire salary or handle all administrative work. A receptionist who answers the sales call is not an obstacle to manipulate into handing over the owner's personal number.

### Distrust is a reasonable starting position

[Banco Pichincha's telephone-fraud guidance](https://www.pichincha.com/blog/vishing-estafas-telefonicas), updated in October 2025, warns about invented identities, callers possessing some personal information, pressure and requests for sensitive information or payments.

Inference: an unfamiliar commercial caller should expect skepticism. Naming public details about the clinic is not proof of legitimacy. Do not imitate a known person, imply an affiliation, manufacture urgency, request access codes or promise that a realistic voice establishes trust. Allow independent verification through a genuine published channel; do not invent one for this prototype.

### Generic AI promises already have competition

[ClaudIA's Ecuador-targeted product page](https://www.soyclaudiadental.com/ec) advertises automated messaging, appointments and out-of-hours coverage. This is evidence that those claims are being marketed to the region, not verification of the vendor's performance, customer count or local adoption.

Implication: “AI that books appointments 24/7” is not by itself a distinctive claim. Do not assert competitors are only recordings or cannot handle corrections. The proposed distinction must be demonstrated in the quality of an actual phone conversation and the correctness of its result.

### Permission and health-data boundaries apply before persuasion

[ARCOTEL's published guidance](https://www.arcotel.gob.ec/arcotel-expide-normativa-que-exige-la-identificacion-de-llamadas-comerciales-publicitarias-o-proselitistas-realizadas-a-traves-de-las-operadoras-moviles/) states that commercial calls require prior express authorization and sets caller-identification requirements; its [implementation FAQ](https://www.arcotel.gob.ec/preguntas-identificacion-llamadas-comerciales/) distinguishes commercial promotions from narrowly informational calls. Treat consent and applicable identification requirements as launch gates to confirm with qualified local advice, not objections to overcome. A public business number is not evidence of permission.

The [SPDP's published 2026 consultation material](https://spdp.gob.ec/consultas2026/) discusses health information as sensitive data. For this exercise, use fictional administrative details only. Calling something a demo does not make real patient information harmless. No medical advice, patient-record imports or clinical histories belong in this sales test.

Scope limits: these sources do not establish national preferences, willingness to pay, telephone demand, lost revenue or an optimal opening. No claim about all Ecuadorians, accents, regional personality or typical clinic income is justified. Start with clear, respectful Spanish and usted; follow the recipient's register without performing local slang.

## 2. Candidate elimination from the recipient's position

| Candidate idea | Plausible skeptical reaction | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everyone calls simultaneously and receives exclusive attention | “That doesn't happen here. What are you selling?” | Reject: implausible setup, unclear offer, capacity unproved. |
| More appointments from the same advertising budget | “You don't know my advertising or why patients don't book.” | Reject: unsupported diagnosis and generic agency language. |
| The AI reveals it is selling itself | “I understood it was a robot. How does this help me?” | Reject: product novelty displaces the buyer's task; explicitly rejected by the operator. |
| Replace reception with an artificial employee | “Who collects payments and deals with the people in front of us?” | Reject: wrong scope and unwanted human persona. |
| An appointment system patients can speak to by phone | “What does that do that my WhatsApp or receptionist doesn't?” | Keep as the next hypothesis: a concrete question we can answer or concede. Not a guaranteed winner. |

The retained opening still has risks. “Agenda” may be heard as another calendar to migrate to. “Atiende el teléfono” may be heard as a prerecorded answering service. The next response must clarify the specific concern, not repeat the metaphor more loudly.

## 3. Conversation tree

```text
Consent/source and AI disclosure established
  └─ Short offer; stop and listen
      ├─ Identity, legitimacy or privacy concern → answer it; no sales continuation until resolved
      ├─ “Recording / another bot” → truthful identity + relevant response to actual words
      ├─ “What does it do?” → one concrete administrative interaction
      ├─ “Already use WhatsApp / already covered” → distinguish channel; accept no fit
      ├─ “It would help with X” → narrow to X; do not restart discovery
      │   ├─ Price or integration question → facts first; unknowns remain unknown
      │   └─ Explicit desire to proceed → relevant next step; booking only with confirmation
      └─ Busy, refusal or opt-out → stop; follow-up only when requested and actually supported
```

These are routing decisions, not an ordered script. Mixed replies are normal. “No, we use WhatsApp—but patients keep calling for directions” is not a rejection of every phone use case. “Send me information” is not consent to a meeting. “Yes” can confirm a booking or merely acknowledge hearing; read the preceding question.

## 4. Eight recipient positions and adversarial paths

### A. Busy owner, already satisfied with WhatsApp

Recipient: “Todo lo manejamos por WhatsApp.”

Reply: “No tendría que dejar WhatsApp. Esta propuesta es para las llamadas. Si ese canal ya está bien cubierto, no le aportaría mucho.”

Recipient: “Casi nadie llama.”

Reply: “Entonces no sería una prioridad para su clínica. Gracias por aclararlo.”

If instead: “Sí llaman, pero les decimos que escriban.”

Reply: “Ahí podría encajar: que quien llama pueda resolver su pregunta y reservar sin tener que empezar otra conversación por escrito.”

Do not label the current process a failure. If the recipient says it works well, accept that. Do not suddenly promise WhatsApp automation; the present demo has no WhatsApp integration.

### B. Receptionist who is protecting her time and position

Recipient: “Yo soy la recepcionista. ¿Me quieren reemplazar?”

Reply: “La propuesta cubre ciertas llamadas, no su puesto completo: no cobra, no recibe a quien llega ni coordina todo el trabajo de la clínica.”

Recipient: “La doctora no está.”

Reply: “Gracias. Si no hay una solicitud de contacto pendiente con ella, lo dejamos aquí.”

Do not fabricate a personal relationship, call it a patient matter, ask for private numbers or exploit concern about job loss. If the recipient voluntarily identifies a legitimate business follow-up route, use only the supported, authorized next step.

### C. Owner who thinks this is prerecorded

Recipient, interrupting: “¿Esto es una grabación?”

Reply: “Es una IA, no una persona ni un audio pregrabado. Responde a lo que usted dice.”

If the recipient has already said “Nosotros usamos WhatsApp,” follow with a relevant adjustment rather than a sales restart:

“Por lo que me dijo, WhatsApp ya les funciona. La cuestión sería únicamente si hay llamadas que también quieren resolver.”

Recipient: “A ver, ¿qué le acabo de decir?”

Reply with the actual, brief fact from the transcript: “Que ya atienden por WhatsApp.” If that fact is unavailable or unclear, acknowledge it; never invent a memory to pass the test.

Recipient: “Entonces no me hable de citas; solo quiero información de horarios.”

Reply: “De acuerdo: información de horarios, sin ofrecer reservas.” Continue only with approved facts or the actual remaining question.

What convinces, if anything, is the change in behavior: stop on interruption, answer the new point and respect the narrower scope. A rehearsed denial alone proves nothing. Do not use “say a random color,” trivia, fake laughter, artificial hesitation or an invented employee identity. Do not force “you're already talking to the product.”

### D. Owner burned by previous bots

Recipient: “Ya probé uno. Repetía lo mismo y terminaba complicando al paciente.”

Reply: “Eso sería un fallo, no una mejora. ¿Se quedaba repitiendo preguntas o llegaba a guardar citas equivocadas?”

This question is earned by a stated failure; it is not a generic discovery checklist. If the recipient already gave the failure, do not ask it again.

Recipient: “Pedía otra vez el nombre que ya le habían dado.”

Reply: “Ese dato tendría que conservarse durante la llamada. Si vuelve a pedirlo sin motivo, no está resolviendo el problema que usted plantea.”

If asked “¿Y el suyo no falla?”:

“Puede fallar. Por eso se comprueba qué entendió y qué quedó realmente registrado; no basta con que la voz diga que ya está.”

Do not claim perfect accuracy, untested superiority or a uniquely capable model. The present demo has verification around action records, but that is not a guarantee that every spoken claim is correct.

### E. Interested owner with a concrete use case

Recipient: “Me serviría para las llamadas que entran mientras estamos cobrando.”

Reply: “Entonces lo limitaríamos a esas llamadas: información administrativa y citas. El resto seguiría con su equipo.”

Recipient: “Solo consultas de valoración, no tratamientos.”

Reply: “Solo valoraciones. No se ofrecerían tratamientos ni se decidiría cuál necesita el paciente.”

Recipient: “Bien. ¿Cómo avanzamos?”

Reply: “El siguiente paso sería revisar ese caso y qué agenda utiliza, para saber si se puede conectar. ¿Quiere que reservemos veinte minutos para verlo?”

No new demo is a prerequisite. If they agree, check availability, gather only missing details and read back the proposed booking before asking for explicit confirmation. State before saving that the current reservation is a local demo record. A prospect's agreement to discuss the idea is not authorization to create a booking.

### F. Manager concerned about implementation, prices and patient information

Recipient: “Yo ya tengo agenda. No quiero otro programa.”

Reply: “La propuesta es añadir atención por voz. La demo usa una agenda de prueba; todavía hay que comprobar cómo trabajaría con la suya.”

Do not promise a plug-and-play connection, deny the integration work or imply that the buyer must replace a working system.

Recipient: “¿Se conecta con mi agenda actual?”

Reply: “No puedo confirmarlo sin saber cuál usa. Hoy la demo tiene una agenda de prueba; la conexión con la suya hay que verificarla.”

Recipient: “Uso Google Calendar. ¿Entonces sí?”

Reply: “En esta demo todavía no está conectado. No le voy a presentar esa integración como algo ya instalado.”

Recipient: “¿Cuánto cuesta?”

Reply: “Todavía no hay una tarifa comercial aprobada para esta demo. No tengo un precio cerrado que darle.”

If price is the only requested information, stop there. Do not substitute a meeting request, collect a budget or invent per-call pricing, a free pilot or a return on investment. This is a real commercial-readiness gap, not an objection copywriting can remove.

Recipient: “¿Y si le preguntan qué tratamiento les conviene?”

Reply: “Eso le corresponde al profesional. El alcance propuesto es información administrativa y agenda, no consejo médico.”

Recipient: “¿Dónde quedan los datos?”

Reply for this demo: “La voz o la transcripción se procesan con proveedores de IA. La aplicación guarda aquí la transcripción y los registros. Use datos ficticios.”

The current UI names OpenAI and the selected backend provider. If asked, identify the actual configured providers. Do not promise all processing is local, a specific residency, zero provider retention or production compliance that has not been established.

### G. Recipient suspicious of the number or the caller's knowledge

Recipient: “¿De dónde sacaron mi número?”

Reply only from an actual permission/source record. If a callback was requested, identify that factual request concisely. If the source is unknown, say it cannot be verified and end the call. Do not invent a referral, purchased contact, previous conversation or consent.

Recipient: “No doy datos por teléfono.”

Reply: “No necesita darme datos para conocer la propuesta. Si prefiere no continuar, terminamos aquí.”

Recipient: “¿Y cómo sé que son ustedes?”

Allow independent verification through a real public business channel, if one exists. Do not make a URL up, pressure them to open a link or treat knowledge of the clinic's address as proof. The present project has no verified public commercial verification route established in this exercise.

Recipient: “¿Esto es para venderme algo?”

Reply: “Sí. Es una propuesta comercial de atención telefónica con IA.” Do not reframe it as a survey, technical notice or patient call.

### H. Recipient wants to leave, follow up elsewhere, or stop recording

“Ahora estoy ocupado.” → “Entendido. Lo dejamos aquí.” Only offer another time if the recipient expresses interest in continuing later; don't squeeze in the remaining pitch.

“Mándeme por WhatsApp.” → “Puedo anotar que prefiere WhatsApp; esta demo no envía mensajes.” Record only a requested follow-up and only if that tool is enabled. Do not say something was sent.

“No me interesa.” → “Entendido, gracias por atender.” No second argument and no contact request.

“No vuelvan a llamar.” → stop selling; save the opt-out if enabled. Describe it as a local demo record, not a guarantee of network-wide suppression. Production dialing needs an enforced suppression system before launch.

“¿Me están grabando?” → “Esta demo guarda una transcripción. La aplicación no conserva un archivo de audio, pero la voz se procesa para responder.” This is not the same question as whether the reply is prerecorded.

“No quiero que quede nada guardado.” → stop the conversation. Do not falsely promise that logging is disabled or earlier data has been erased: the existing app has no in-call deletion or no-retention tool. Resolve any deletion request through a supported operator process.

## 5. What makes the demonstration feel responsive

The reveal is ordinary language having an immediate, relevant effect. The patient doesn't have to preserve the system's script. Examples worth testing with consenting participants:

- “No el martes: el jueves, y todavía no reserve.” The date changes, but no appointment is created.
- “El correo ya lo dije.” Reuse it from context rather than requesting it again.
- “Solo quería saber el precio.” Answer price or its genuine absence; abandon the unsolicited booking branch.
- “No quiero dar mi cédula.” Do not demand it; the demo's booking flow does not require it.
- “No era para mí.” Clarify only what the administrative task requires; don't start a medical interview.
- A caller interrupts with “espere.” Stop the spoken pitch instead of finishing the paragraph.

None requires a staged magic trick or a claim that the system can read minds. The system shows attention through the consequences of what it heard. Do not turn these examples into required customer exercises; use them as test cases internally or answer them when the buyer spontaneously raises them.

## 6. Acceptance criteria for the next lab run

Not performed in this research round:

1. Use consenting Ecuadorian participants, including someone who operates reception and someone who owns/manages a service business. Do not coach them toward “¿cómo?” or tell them to admire the voice.
2. Let them hear the opening once. Ask what was being offered and what they would naturally say next. Log actual words, confusion, interruption and refusal; a compliment about the voice is not purchase interest.
3. Compare the retained opening against a plain factual control: “Hola, de Aló. Ofrecemos atención telefónica con IA para responder preguntas y gestionar citas.” Vary delivery only in a separate comparison.
4. Rotate interruption, prerecorded-audio suspicion, WhatsApp sufficiency, previous-bot failure and changed booking intent. Do not count a cooperative scripted booking as success on every branch.
5. Review the audio and authoritative saved records together. A correct text response is not proof of correct playback, natural latency or accurate booking.
6. Hard failures: false human identity, continuing after a clear refusal, invented price/integration, clinical advice, false privacy assurance, claiming an unsaved action succeeded, saving before confirmation, or repeating the pitch after a correction.
7. Commercial relevance failures: participant cannot explain the offer, hears only another calendar to migrate to, or has no meaningful telephone use case. Rewrite or choose a different buyer; don't redefine their lack of need as an objection to defeat.

A small listening test is qualitative feedback, not a conversion-rate study. There are no success percentages to report yet.

## 7. Instructions for any future prompt update

- Keep these as proposed directions until the operator chooses the next version; this file does not modify live prompts.
- Retire the fictional agent name and the rejected self-selling reveal. Product/company identity is separate from inventing an employee.
- Keep research and objection reasoning in Pi; delivery and responsive listening belong to the voice layer.
- Offer one next move based on the recipient's actual last turn. Do not chain every objection response or ask a question automatically.
- Keep the three existing action permissions and their confirmation requirements. No invented WhatsApp, rescheduling, medical, payment or transfer capabilities.
- Describe the current prototype honestly. A prospective production capability is not a feature already available in this browser simulation.
